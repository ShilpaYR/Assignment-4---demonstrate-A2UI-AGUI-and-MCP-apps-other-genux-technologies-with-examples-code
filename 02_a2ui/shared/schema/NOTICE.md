# Schema files

`agent_to_renderer.json`, `renderer_to_agent.json`, `common_types.json`,
`catalog_definition.json` and `basic_catalog.json` are copied unchanged from the
official A2UI repository (https://github.com/google/A2UI, `specification/v1_0/json/`
and `catalogs/basic/v1/catalog.json`, commit a23b19c, Nov 2026). They are licensed
under the Apache License 2.0, Copyright Google LLC.

`genux_html_catalog.json` is written for this course: a custom catalog (allowed by
A2UI v1.0, which lets one surface mix catalogs through `@catalogId`).
